---
title: Worry Time Technique
category: cbt
tags: anxiety, overthinking, rumination
---
"Worry time" (also called scheduled worry) is a CBT technique for people whose anxious thoughts intrude throughout the day. Rather than suppressing worry — which tends to backfire — it contains it to a specific window.

How to do it:
1. Set aside a fixed 15-minute "worry time" at the same time each day, not close to bedtime.
2. When a worry pops up outside that window, briefly note it ("money," "the meeting tomorrow") and consciously postpone it: "I'll think about this at 6pm."
3. Return attention to the current task.
4. During worry time, actually go through the list — think it through, problem-solve where possible, and let go of what you can't control.

Over time this trains the brain to trust that worries will be addressed, reducing the urge to ruminate on them constantly, and often people find the worry has lost intensity by the time worry time arrives.
