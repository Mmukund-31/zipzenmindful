---
title: Building Healthy Routines
category: habits
tags: routine, habits, motivation, structure
---
Routines reduce the mental load of decision-making and give the nervous system predictability, which is especially stabilizing during periods of stress, low mood, or big life change.

Tips for building one that sticks:
- Anchor new habits to existing ones ("after I brush my teeth, I'll do 2 minutes of stretching") rather than relying on willpower alone.
- Start smaller than feels necessary — a 2-minute version of the habit is more sustainable than an ambitious version you abandon after 3 days.
- Track completion simply (a checkmark on a calendar) — visible progress is motivating on its own.
- Expect and plan for missed days; consistency over months matters far more than a perfect streak, and missing one day rarely undoes progress unless it turns into missing many in a row.

A basic daily structure — consistent wake time, some movement, at least one intentional wellness moment — is one of the most protective factors for mood over time.
