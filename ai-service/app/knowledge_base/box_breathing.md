Box breathing (also called square breathing) is a simple technique used by Navy SEALs and therapists alike to calm the nervous system in under a minute.

How to do it:
1. Inhale slowly through your nose for a count of 4.
2. Hold your breath for a count of 4.
3. Exhale slowly through your mouth for a count of 4.
4. Hold the empty breath for a count of 4.
5. Repeat the cycle 4 times.

Box breathing works by activating the parasympathetic nervous system, which slows the heart rate and signals safety to the brain. It is especially effective right before a stressful event (a difficult conversation, a presentation, a wave of panic) because it can be done discreetly, anywhere, with no equipment.
