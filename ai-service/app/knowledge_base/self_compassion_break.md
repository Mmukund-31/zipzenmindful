---
title: Self-Compassion Break
category: self-compassion
tags: self-criticism, shame, kindness
---
Developed by psychologist Dr. Kristin Neff, the self-compassion break is a short practice for moments of failure, criticism, or emotional pain, built on three components: mindfulness, common humanity, and self-kindness.

How to do it, silently or aloud:
1. Mindfulness: "This is a moment of suffering" or "This is really hard right now" — naming the pain without exaggerating or denying it.
2. Common humanity: "Suffering is part of being human" or "I'm not alone in feeling this way" — remembering that struggle connects rather than isolates.
3. Self-kindness: "May I be kind to myself" or "What do I need right now?" — offering yourself the same warmth you'd offer a friend.

This takes under a minute and directly counters the harsh inner critic that intensifies shame and self-blame after a mistake or setback.
