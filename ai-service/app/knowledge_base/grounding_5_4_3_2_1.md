---
title: 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Technique
category: grounding
tags: anxiety, panic, dissociation, overthinking
---
The 5-4-3-2-1 technique uses your five senses to pull your attention out of anxious or spiraling thoughts and back into the present moment. It is one of the most widely used grounding exercises in CBT and trauma-informed therapy.

How to do it, naming out loud or in your head:
- 5 things you can SEE around you
- 4 things you can TOUCH or feel (the texture of your sleeve, the chair beneath you)
- 3 things you can HEAR
- 2 things you can SMELL
- 1 thing you can TASTE

Go slowly and really notice each item rather than rushing through the list. This technique is particularly effective for panic attacks, dissociation, and racing or repetitive thoughts, because it forces the brain to process concrete sensory input instead of the anxious narrative.
