---
title: 4-7-8 Breathing
category: breathing
tags: sleep, anxiety, relaxation
---
The 4-7-8 technique, popularized by Dr. Andrew Weil, is designed to shift the body out of "fight or flight" and into rest mode, making it especially useful before sleep or during a spike of anxiety.

How to do it:
1. Exhale completely through your mouth, making a whoosh sound.
2. Close your mouth and inhale quietly through your nose for a count of 4.
3. Hold your breath for a count of 7.
4. Exhale completely through your mouth for a count of 8, making a whoosh sound.
5. Repeat for 4 breath cycles.

The extended exhale is the key mechanism: longer exhales than inhales lower heart rate and blood pressure. New practitioners may feel lightheaded at first — that's normal, and the effect fades with practice.
