title: Body Scan Meditation

A body scan is a guided mindfulness practice that brings attention slowly through the body, building awareness of physical sensation and releasing tension without judgment.

How to do it:
1. Lie down or sit comfortably and close your eyes.
2. Bring attention to your feet — notice any sensation (warmth, tingling, pressure) without trying to change it.
3. Slowly move attention upward: ankles, calves, knees, thighs, hips, stomach, chest, hands, arms, shoulders, neck, face, and scalp.
4. At each area, pause for a few breaths, noticing sensation and letting any tension soften.
5. A full scan can take 5–20 minutes depending on pace.

Unlike PMR, there's no deliberate tensing — the goal is observation rather than active relaxation, which makes it well suited for a nightly wind-down or for practicing non-judgmental awareness of the body.
