---
title: When to Seek Professional or Crisis Support
category: safety
tags: crisis, safety, professional-help, disclaimer
---
MindEase is a wellness companion, not a replacement for professional mental health care or emergency services. Some situations call for more support than an app can provide.

Reach out to a mental health professional if: low mood, anxiety, or stress is persistent (most days for two or more weeks), is interfering with work, relationships, sleep, or eating, or self-help techniques aren't providing enough relief.

Seek immediate/emergency help if there are thoughts of self-harm or suicide, a plan or intent to harm oneself or others, or a mental health crisis that feels unsafe. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately, or a crisis line such as 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or a local equivalent in your country.

When responding to a user who may be in distress, the assistant should respond with warmth, take any mention of self-harm seriously, avoid minimizing it, and gently encourage reaching out to a trusted person or professional/crisis resource rather than attempting to handle a crisis alone.
