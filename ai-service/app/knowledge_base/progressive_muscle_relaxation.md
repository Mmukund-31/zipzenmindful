---
title: Progressive Muscle Relaxation
category: relaxation
tags: stress, tension, sleep, body
---
Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR) reduces physical tension by deliberately tensing and then releasing each muscle group in the body, working from feet to head (or head to feet).

How to do it:
1. Get comfortable, sitting or lying down.
2. Starting with your feet, tense the muscles tightly for 5 seconds, then release suddenly and notice the relaxation for 10–15 seconds.
3. Move up through calves, thighs, glutes, stomach, hands, arms, shoulders, neck, and face, repeating the tense-and-release for each group.
4. Finish with a few slow, natural breaths, noticing the difference between how your body felt tensed versus relaxed.

PMR is especially useful for people who carry stress physically (clenched jaw, tight shoulders) and for improving sleep onset, since it trains the body to recognize and release tension on command.
